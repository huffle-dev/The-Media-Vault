Row 1 stays at 6 tabs (All + 5 categories) forever, no matter how many concrete or custom types exist — a new custom type just slots into Other (or you could let it join an existing category later; kept simple here). Clicking a category reveals a second, smaller sub-type row scoped to just that group, so within "Play" you're choosing between 4 tabs, not 14. This is the same interaction depth as today (2 clicks to reach a specific type: category → sub-type) — it's actually fewer total visible tabs than the current 8, while scaling to any number of future types without redesigning again.
Trade-off worth naming: a specific type is now one click deeper (category, then type) instead of one flat click. For someone who lives in "Board Games" specifically, that's a small regression versus today's single click — the win is entirely about not degrading as more types get added.

Sizing rule that applies everywhere on row 2
Every control — Filter, Country, Sort, the divider, Online Search, Local Search — is exactly 26px tall, matching Country. This required setting height explicitly on both the buttons and their position:relative wrapper divs (Filter/Sort) — the wrapper alone can end up a few px taller than its button due to ordinary inline-formatting line-height slack, which throws things out of level even when every visible button reports the same 26px.
