# Changelog

What changed in each release of The Media Vault. The newest release is at the top.

## 1.0.0

The first public release.

- **Desktop app (Windows):** a personal library for movies, TV, books, audiobooks, games, music, board games, podcasts, web videos and websites, with lists, stats, history, discovery and search by title or creator.
- **Phone app (Android):** the same library on your phone, read and edited live from your own Supabase project, with offline saving, adding by search, barcode or share, and a cover upload.
- **Your own server:** sync runs through a Supabase project you create; nothing is sent to anyone else. The setup guide walks through it, including a QR code that sets up the phone.
- **Free and open source** under the MIT licence.
