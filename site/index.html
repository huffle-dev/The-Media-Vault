What it does

Everything you keep track of, in one library that follows you between your computer and your phone.

Every kind of thing

Search the internet for covers and details, scan a barcode, share a link from another app, or add things by hand.

See where you are

Wishlist, not started, in progress, consumed, dropped. Rate things, keep lists and favourites, and look back at what you finished and when.

Find what's next

Discover suggests things based on what you already like, and Where to Watch shows which streaming service has a title.

Works offline

Save your whole library, covers included, to the phone and browse it with no connection.

Your data stays yours

The library syncs through a free Supabase project that you create. There's no account with us, no tracking and no adverts.

Open source

Released under the MIT licence. Read it, change it, build it yourself, or tell us what's broken.

Getting started takes about ten minutes

You only need an account for the free server that holds your library, and it's yours alone.

  1. Install the Windows appDownload it from the latest release and run it.
  2. Make your free serverCreate a free Supabase project and paste in the setup file the app gives you. The setup guide walks through every click.
  3. Create your account in the appNo separate sign-up page. The app does it for you.
  4. Scan one code with your phoneThe app shows a QR code; the Android app reads it and signs in.
